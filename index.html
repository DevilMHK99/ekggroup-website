Who we are

A family partnership, three decades in the field.

EKG Group is a family office. We invest our own capital across the real estate spectrum: assembling and master-planning land, developing prime residential, mixed-use and commercial assets, and, increasingly, large-scale digital infrastructure.

We concentrate where execution is hard, on sites that demand utility, transmission, planning and stakeholder work other developers will not take on. We build them into something coherent and lasting, and we operate what we deliver rather than simply trading it. Because we hold our assets for decades rather than trading them, building responsibly is simply how the capital is protected.

What we do

Placemaking, land, and long-term development.

We take on land that is complex to assemble and slow to mature: dormant parcels, powered sites, difficult schemes. We master-plan, build and operate them as places, applying the same discipline whether the outcome is housing, hospitality, or digital infrastructure.

Assembling and master-planning land into coherent, mixed-use communities, returning dormant and underused sites to productive use, designed around the people who will live and work there.

The signature

Power first.

The scarcest input in modern development is not land or capital. It is deliverable power. We assemble sites that already sit on high-voltage transmission, then master-plan around it, so digital infrastructure, housing and jobs can be brought forward together without straining the surrounding grid.

It is patient, difficult work: utility, planning and community, in the open, over years. It is exactly the work we have built the group to do.
